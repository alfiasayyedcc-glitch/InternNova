# Privacy Policy for InternNova

**Last Updated: August 16, 2026**

InternNova ("we", "our", or "the App") is an internship management and discovery application designed to help students find and manage internship opportunities and help mentors/companies manage internship-related activities.

This Privacy Policy explains how InternNova collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the application.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, InternNova may collect the following information:

### Account Information
When you create or use an account, we may collect:
- Name
- Email address
- Password or authentication information
- User role, such as Student, Mentor, or Admin

### Student Information
Students may provide:
- College or educational information
- Skills and qualifications
- Internship preferences
- Internship applications
- Tasks and internship progress
- Profile information
- Documents or reports uploaded through the application, where supported

### Mentor/Company Information
Mentors or companies may provide:
- Name
- Organization/company information
- Contact information
- Internship opportunities
- Internship descriptions
- Tasks and assignments
- Feedback and evaluation information

### Application Data
The application may store information related to:
- Internship listings
- Internship applications
- Application status
- Assigned tasks
- Reports
- Feedback
- Internship progress

## 2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- Create and manage user accounts
- Provide internship discovery and application features
- Allow students to apply for internships
- Allow mentors/companies to manage internships
- Assign and manage internship tasks
- Track internship progress
- Provide feedback and reports
- Improve the functionality and reliability of the application
- Maintain application security
- Respond to user requests and support inquiries

We do not use personal information for purposes unrelated to the operation of InternNova without appropriate notice or consent.

## 3. Firebase Services

InternNova may use Firebase services provided by Google for authentication, database storage, and application functionality.

Depending on the features enabled in the application, Firebase may process information such as account information and application data.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent users' personal information.

Information may be accessible to authorized users where necessary for the application's functionality. For example, information submitted as part of an internship application may be available to the relevant mentor or company.

We may also use trusted service providers, such as Firebase, to provide infrastructure and application services.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's services, maintain records, comply with applicable requirements, and resolve disputes.

Users may request deletion of their account or personal information where applicable.

## 7. Children's Privacy

InternNova is intended for students and other users who are legally permitted to use the application.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that the information can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate.

## 8. User Rights

Depending on applicable laws, users may have rights to:

- Access their personal information
- Correct inaccurate information
- Request deletion of their information
- Withdraw consent where applicable
- Request information about how their data is used

Requests may be submitted using the contact information provided below.

## 9. Third-Party Services

InternNova may use third-party services necessary for application functionality.

These services may have their own privacy policies and terms. Users are encouraged to review the privacy policies of third-party services used by the application.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.

## 11. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of personal information, please contact the InternNova team.
